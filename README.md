# Enacted: Federal Regulatory & Statutory Repository (`enacted-federal`)

This repository contains verified primary-source records of federal statutory law and administrative regulations, including Safe Drinking Water Act National Primary Drinking Water Regulations and Federal Register rulemaking notices.

**Parent Entity:** Swapp Technologies LLC (Organized in Maryland)  
**Repository Scope:** Federal Environmental & Land-Use Law  
**Posture:** This repository records the enacted law. It does not advise, approve, determine, or certify anything.  

---

## 1. What This Repository Is

Enacted records federal statutory mandates, agency rulemakings, and regulatory compliance chronologies directly from primary government instruments:
1. **Verbatim Federal Instruments**: Mechanically extracted provisions and rulemaking notices from the Federal Register, Code of Federal Regulations, and United States Code.
2. **Deterministic Timelines**: Compliance deadlines, proposed extension notices, and effective dates.
3. **Primary Federal Contacts**: Sponsoring agency divisions, dockets, and public comment mechanisms.

### Standard Verification Line
*This repository records the enacted law. It does not advise, approve, determine, or certify anything.*  
All text quotes, reports, shows, and records primary government documents. It does not provide legal advice or apply law to specific facts.

---

## 2. Sourcing Standards

All records are sourced exclusively from official federal repositories:
* **GovInfo** (`govinfo.gov` / U.S. Government Publishing Office)
* **Federal Register** (`federalregister.gov` / National Archives and Records Administration)
* **United States Code** (`uscode.house.gov` / Office of the Law Revision Counsel)

Third-party aggregators, commentary, and non-government websites are strictly prohibited.
