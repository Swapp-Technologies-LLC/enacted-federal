# EPA Proposed Rule — Extending the Compliance Deadline for the PFOA and PFOS Maximum Contaminant Levels (91 FR 29425)

## 1. Agency Rulemaking Summary
- **Agency:** Environmental Protection Agency (EPA)
- **Document Number:** 2026-10086 (Citation: 91 FR 29425)
- **Publication Date:** May 20, 2026
- **Action:** Notice of proposed rulemaking; request for public comment; notice of public hearing
- **Dockets:** EPA-HQ-OW-2025-1742; FRL 8543.1-01-OW
- **Governing Standard:** Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs) for Perfluorooctanoic acid (PFOA) and Perfluorooctanesulfonic acid (PFOS) under 40 CFR Part 141 / 142.
- **Proposed Modification:** Proposes extending the compliance deadline for public water systems to comply with the federal Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs) for PFOA and PFOS by two years, to April 26, 2031 for eligible systems that apply for an exemption under SDWA §1416(f).

## 2. Verbatim Quoted Rulemaking Text
From Federal Register Vol. 91, No. 97 (Wednesday, May 20, 2026 / Proposed Rules, page 29425):
```
ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY
40 CFR Part 142
[EPA–HQ–OW–2025–1742; FRL 8543.1–01–OW]
RIN 2040–AG38
Extending the Compliance Deadline for the PFOA and PFOS Maximum Contaminant Levels
AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency (EPA).
ACTION: Notice of proposed rulemaking; request for public comment; notice of public hearing.
SUMMARY: The Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) is proposing to provide public water systems (PWSs) additional time to comply with the federal Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs) for perfluorooctanoic acid (PFOA) and perfluorooctanesulfonic acid (PFOS).
DATES: Comments must be received on or before July 20, 2026. Under the Paperwork Reduction Act (PRA), comments on the information collection provisions are best assured of consideration if the Office of Management and Budget (OMB) receives a copy of your comments on or before June 22, 2026.
Public hearing: The EPA will hold a virtual public hearing on July 7, 2026.
```
